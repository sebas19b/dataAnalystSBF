# Billing Management Dashboard

The goal of this dashboard was to perform a descriptive analysis of billing, examining how sales trend over time and some of their key metrics.

## What questions does it answer?

How do sales trend over the months?
Which month has the highest billing?
Which month has the lowest billing?
How do payment methods trend?
What is the average transaction amount?
What is the total discount recorded?

## One of the initial findings

When analyzing cumulative revenue over the different years, March shows the highest sales, while September shows the lowest.

Regarding payment methods, the share of bank transfers and cash remains relatively close during these periods, with no significant difference observed.
