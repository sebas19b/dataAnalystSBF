# 📊 Customer and Sales Dashboard — Power BI

##  Project Description

This project involves the development of a dashboard in **Power BI** designed to analyze sales and customer behavior.

The main objective is to transform the available data into useful information to identify business patterns, analyze customer behavior, and detect opportunities that can support decision-making.

##  Objective

Analyze various business metrics to answer questions such as:

* What is the average ticket?
* How are sales distributed by region?
* What is the average sales per customer?
* How many customers can be considered active?
* How many customers have not made a purchase in over two years?
* What business opportunities can be identified based on these results?

---

## 📊 Metrics Analyzed

###  Average Transaction Value

This metric reveals the average value of each purchase made.

###  Sales by Region

This metric helps identify how sales are distributed geographically and detect differences between regions.

###  Average Sales Per Customer

This metric allows for an analysis of the average value generated by each customer.

###  Active customers

For this project, an active customer was defined as one who made **at least one purchase in the last two years**, using the current date as a reference.

###  Inactive Customers

An inactive customer is defined as one whose **last purchase occurred more than two years ago** relative to the current date.

This definition allows us to distinguish between customers who have been active recently and those who might represent an opportunity for reactivation.

---

## 🔎 Analysis Approach

The project follows a business analysis-oriented structure:

**Data → Metrics → Findings → Interpretation → Decision**

The goal is not merely to present charts, but to use the metrics to identify situations that may require business action.

---

## 💡 Key Identifiable Opportunities

Based on the analysis, opportunities such as the following can be identified:

* Design reactivation strategies for inactive customers.
* Prioritize customers with the highest historical value.
* Analyze regions with lower sales levels.
* Review purchase frequency and customer behavior.
* Use the metrics to help define future business strategies.
